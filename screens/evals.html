4. Known Failures & Operational Edge Cases

mock mode

Transparent telemetry of edge-case failure modes and active system mitigations under crisis conditions.

bug_report
water_dropFlood Noise & Mixed Dialect

Mixed dialects with heavy background flood rush noise can drop landmark street numbers during live call streaming.

Active Mitigation

Automated SMS dispatch with one-tap GPS fallback sharing coordinates directly to GIS vehicle assignment array.

record_voice_overRapid Distress Cross-Talk

Rapid multi-speaker cross-talk during distress calls can cause partial entity dropping (e.g. child headcount merged with adult count).

Active Mitigation

Incident queued into field worker follow-up roster with high-priority audio review playback badge.

translateTulu/Kannada Code-Mix Idioms

Code-mixed Tulu/Kannada regional idioms occasionally misclassify second room or attic locations within waterlogged structures.

Active Mitigation

Standard dropdown verification triggered on rescue operator mobile interface during vehicle assignment.
