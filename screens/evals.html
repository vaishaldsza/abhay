4. Known Failures & Operational Edge Cases

mock mode

Transparent telemetry of edge-case failure modes and active system mitigations under crisis conditions.

bug_report
water_dropFlood Noise & Mixed Dialect

Mixed dialects with heavy background flood rush noise can drop landmark street numbers during live call streaming.

Active Mitigation

Automated SMS dispatch with one-tap GPS fallback sharing coordinates directly to GIS vehicle assignment array.

record_voice_overRapid Distress Cross-Talk

Rapid multi-speaker cross-talk during distress calls can cause partial entity dropping (e.g. child headcount merged with adult count).

Active Mitigation

Incident queued into field worker follow-up roster with high-priority audio review playback badge.

translateTulu/Kannada Code-Mix Idioms

Code-mixed Tulu/Kannada regional idioms occasionally misclassify second room or attic locations within waterlogged structures.

Active Mitigation

Standard dropdown verification triggered on rescue operator mobile interface during vehicle assignment.

verified_user Strict Methodological & Provenance Disclaimers:
  • On-time % denominator: On-time % is strictly computed over ALL households needing a ride (24 households). Unassigned households count as NOT evacuated on time.
  • Extreme scenario is dominated by seat shortage: With only 3 vehicles (12 seats) for ~72 people, ~20 households remain unassigned regardless of algorithm. On-time pickups tie with FCFS (median 8.3% of households, 5–6 people).
  • Moderate scenario has loose deadlines: With 45–120 min water arrival deadlines, virtually every assigned household arrives on time (~70.8% of households, ~51 people).
  • Routing gain is about 1% of assigned-plan cost: In the synthetic data, the optimizer reduces assigned-plan transit/risk cost by approximately 1% compared to greedy ($4,088.7$ vs $4,131.4$ pts).
  • Cost advantage is over greedy only: The optimizer's cost reduction is strictly relative to the Priority Greedy heuristic baseline, not over FCFS or all dispatch heuristics in general.
  • Single-trip vehicles: All scenarios are synthetic with single-trip vehicles (no return loops or second trips).
  • Deadlines are demo assumptions: All water arrival minutes, transit speeds (25 km/h), and boarding buffers are synthetic demo parameters, not calibrated hydrodynamic models.
  • Hand-built test provenance: Hand-built test cases were written by the same agent that changed the optimizer operators. The optimizer does not materially improve evacuation outcomes in general.
